Placement
Your pen mount sits this far toward the front of the toolhead. Y0 is the front of the plate (Bambu convention), so the pen can't reach the back strip of the plate — the toolhead would have to travel past its rear limit. That strip is excluded from the drawable area, and every Y sent to the printer is shifted (+offset) so the pen lands where the artwork is, not the nozzle.
Pen (Z-lift)
Setup / swap hold time 30s
How long the pen holds still, lowered onto the paper at the artwork corner, before plotting begins — and again before each colour swap. Give yourself more time if you need to fiddle with a tight mount; less if you're already dialled in and just want to get plotting.
Setup corner offset (mm) 0
Moves the setup and swap hold position this many mm in front of and to the left of the artwork. Any ink dot from the hold lands on waste paper or bare plate instead of the drawing.
Nozzle setting: this file is generated for a 0.4 nozzle. Set the printer itself to 0.4 (A1 screen → settings → nozzle) and Bambu Studio's profile to "Bambu Lab A1 0.4 nozzle" or the printer will refuse to start. The pen doesn't care — this is metadata only.
Every plot starts by homing all axes (G28) so the origin is always true. Caution: the A1 homes Z by touching the nozzle to the plate — if your pen tip sits below the nozzle, use a spring-loaded mount or fit the pen after homing completes (pause at the pen-swap prompt on multi-colour plots).
Before running: jog the pen down by hand and set your Z offset so "pen down" just touches the paper — treat that as Z0 here.
